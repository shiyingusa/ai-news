## Users

Technology professionals, engineers and researchers, founders and investors, and policy and industry watchers who need to understand what changed in frontier technology today without wading through hype. English-speaking and Chinese-speaking readers are equally important (owner decision, 2026-11-16): both languages get first-class editions, navigation and email, not a translated afterthought. Typical scene: a morning read on a laptop or phone, ten minutes, deciding what matters for their work.

## Product Purpose

AI Daily Radar picks the few frontier-technology developments that matter each day across AI, compute, robotics, energy and mobility, space and science, and industry and policy; merges every report of one event into a single story; separates what the evidence establishes from what companies claim; and connects stories into longer technology and industry threads (companies, technologies, timelines). Success: a reader understands within five seconds what the site is, what today's key changes are and why the morning email is worth subscribing to; readers return daily; facts are traceable; corrections are public.

## Positioning

"The Intelligence Layer for Frontier Technology" / 全球前沿科技情报与产业分析. The mechanism a neighbouring news site cannot truthfully copy: every story carries a status label (Rumored, Planned, Announced, Demonstrated, Released, Available), a verified-versus-claimed evidence block, a credited original publisher, and a place in an event cluster and entity timeline, in two languages built from one fact record.

## Constraints

- Licensed images only (Wikimedia Commons free licences, Pixabay/Pexels); generated images must be labeled illustrative.

## Brand commitments

- Name: AI Daily Radar / AI 每日雷达. Existing radar-ring logo mark (orange dot) may be kept or redrawn.
