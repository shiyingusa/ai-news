<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier and above the $109.1 billion analyst estimate cited by Techmeme. Data Center revenue was $107.6 billion, up 110% year over year, meaning data-center chips accounted for roughly 93% of the quarter's sales. The company also guided for fourth-quarter revenue above estimates, though the source material does not give the guidance figure. It was the first earnings call with CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones as the company's first board chair, following Jensen Huang's resignation the previous week; Jones said the board "does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO" and that Nvidia would not address questions about the circumstances of the resignation or an ongoing investigation.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Nvidia's own results release, as summarized by Techmeme, reports $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue (up 103% year over year), $107.6 billion in Data Center revenue (up 110%), and Q4 guidance above estimates. The Verge reports that Kress is interim CEO, Jones is board chair, and that Jones told the call the board does not intend to reinstate Huang.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The exact Q4 revenue guidance figure is not given in the supplied material, so how far it exceeds estimates cannot be checked. The Verge, citing reports, says Huang resigned after reports of multiple affairs, including one with a former Megaspeed executive described as an alleged Chinese asset; Nvidia has not discussed the circumstances, and the company says it is investigating whether that relationship compromised national security or company secrets — these are allegations and an open inquiry, not established findings.

</div>

## Background

These results follow the supply picture Nvidia disclosed in its prior-quarter SEC filing: AI Daily Radar's November 16 digest reported that the filing showed $530 billion in gross off-balance-sheet guarantees, up from $184 billion the quarter before, with supply and capacity commitments rising by nearly a quarter of a trillion dollars in a single quarter, mostly for memory. The quarter is also Nvidia's first earnings call since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO the previous week; CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO alongside new board chair Harvey C. Jones, and the board said it does not intend to reinstate Huang while an investigation into his conduct continues. Huang is separately scheduled to testify before lawmakers on December 1.

## Why it matters

For anyone buying AI accelerators, the results confirm that spending on data-center compute is still accelerating rather than plateauing: Data Center alone grew 110% year over year to $107.6 billion in a single quarter, and guidance above estimates signals Nvidia expects demand to keep outpacing expectations into the fourth quarter. The concentration is also a supply-chain risk — with roughly 93% of revenue coming from one segment served by a limited set of contract manufacturers and memory suppliers, buyers' delivery schedules remain tied to Nvidia's ability to keep that pipeline expanding.

## What to watch

Huang is scheduled to testify before lawmakers on December 1, per The Verge, which should clarify the circumstances of his resignation and the scope of Nvidia's internal investigation. Nvidia's next earnings report will also show whether fourth-quarter revenue actually lands above the guidance it gave.
