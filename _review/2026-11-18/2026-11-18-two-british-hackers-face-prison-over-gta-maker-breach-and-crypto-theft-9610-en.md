TechCrunch reported on November 18 that two British hackers face lengthy prison sentences for breaking into the maker of Grand Theft Auto and for stealing millions in cryptocurrency. The report, by Zack Whittaker, covers both cases in a single sentence and does not name the defendants, the charges, the court, or the amount taken. It is not clear from the report whether the two have been convicted and are awaiting sentencing, or whether the allegations are still being contested.

## Background

The GTA 6 breach traces back to 2022, when unreleased development footage and source material from Rockstar Games appeared online. Eurogamer reported that the leaker in that case pleaded guilty to hacking charges and was initially sentenced to an indefinite stay in a hospital rather than prison, later being moved to a regular prison after being judged fit for trial. The current report does not say whether the two men now facing prison sentences are the same people.
