# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news about robots and autonomous vehicles for readers who follow how AI is moving into the physical world.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Robotaxi or humanoid deployments at real scale, major safety rulings or recalls, or capability results that change what robots can do.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable robot or autonomy launches, city expansions, significant funding, credible demonstrations with evidence, or important incident investigations.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental updates, useful products, moderate interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rehashed coverage of older news, shallow takes, rumours without sourcing, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Off-topic or noise.** Items whose primary subject is not robots or autonomous machines, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

The primary subject must be robots, drones or autonomous driving; other technology news scores 0-2 here. Staged demos without evidence of real capability should not score above 6. Consider novelty, likely real-world impact, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Prefer primary sources (official announcements, filings, papers, regulator documents, release notes) over re-reporting of the same event. Distinguish what has shipped or been measured from what is announced, rumoured or forecast. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype, stock-price chatter or exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
