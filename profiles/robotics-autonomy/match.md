# Robots & Self-Driving profile

Use this profile for news whose primary subject is robots or autonomous machines: humanoid robots (including Tesla Optimus), industrial and service robots, robot foundation models, drones, robotaxis and self-driving systems (Waymo, Tesla FSD, Zoox, Baidu Apollo and others), and the safety rules that govern them.

Do not use this profile for general EV sales or battery news, AI news without a physical robot or vehicle, or toy and gadget promotions.
