# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news for readers who follow artificial intelligence: builders, product people, creators, and informed general readers who want to know what changed in AI today and why it matters.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Frontier model releases, major capability or research breakthroughs, landmark AI regulation, or industry-changing announcements from major AI labs and companies.
- **7-8: High value.** Important AI developments worth prompt attention: notable model or product updates, significant open-source releases, credible evaluations, major funding or partnerships, or insightful analysis tied to a recent event.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental AI updates, useful tools, moderate community interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rehashed coverage of older news, shallow takes, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Off-topic or noise.** Items whose primary subject is not artificial intelligence, spam, trivial updates, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

The primary subject must be AI. General technology, software, security, or business news scores 0-2 unless AI is central to the story. Consider novelty, likely impact on people who use or build AI, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Prefer primary sources (official announcements, papers, release notes) over re-reporting of the same event. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype or exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
