# AI news profile

Use this profile for timely developments whose primary subject is artificial intelligence: model releases and updates, AI products and features, AI research results, AI infrastructure and chips, AI policy and regulation, AI company and funding news, AI safety incidents, and notable open-source AI tools.

Typical items include launches from AI labs, benchmark or evaluation results, pricing and availability changes for AI services, government actions on AI, and community discussions that add practical insight into using or building with AI.

Do not use this profile for technology news where AI is only a passing mention, general software or hardware news without an AI angle, long-form tutorials, full academic papers, finance-first market coverage, or promotional material without substantive information.
