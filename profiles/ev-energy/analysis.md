# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news about EVs, batteries and clean energy for readers who follow Tesla and the technology shift in transport and power.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Battery or energy breakthroughs with credible evidence, major policy changes, landmark plant or reactor milestones, or company results and strategy shifts that move the whole EV industry.
- **7-8: High value.** Important vehicle launches or recalls, quarterly deliveries and pricing changes at major EV makers, significant battery, storage, solar or nuclear projects, or insightful analysis tied to a recent event.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental updates, useful products, moderate interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rehashed coverage of older news, shallow takes, rumours without sourcing, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Off-topic or noise.** Items whose primary subject is not EVs, batteries or energy, stock-price speculation without news, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

The primary subject must be EVs, batteries, charging or energy; other technology news scores 0-2 here. Rumours and fan speculation about Tesla should score 4 or lower unless confirmed by the company, filings or regulators. Consider novelty, likely real-world impact, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Prefer primary sources (official announcements, filings, papers, regulator documents, release notes) over re-reporting of the same event. Distinguish what has shipped or been measured from what is announced, rumoured or forecast. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype, stock-price chatter or exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
