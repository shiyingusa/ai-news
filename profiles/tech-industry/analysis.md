# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely technology-industry and policy news for readers who follow frontier technology.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Landmark regulation or court rulings, very large acquisitions, or events that reshape a major part of the technology industry.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable strategy shifts, significant funding or layoffs at leading companies, important policy proposals, and major security incidents with real impact.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental updates, useful products, moderate interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rehashed coverage of older news, shallow takes, rumours without sourcing, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Off-topic or noise.** Items that are not about the technology industry, consumer deals, gadget rumours, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Keep the bar high: this profile exists for the few industry and policy stories every technology reader should know. Consider novelty, likely real-world impact, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Prefer primary sources (official announcements, filings, papers, regulator documents, release notes) over re-reporting of the same event. Distinguish what has shipped or been measured from what is announced, rumoured or forecast. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype, stock-price chatter or exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
