# Tech Industry & Policy profile

Use this profile for major technology-industry and policy news that does not fit a more specific profile: big-tech strategy, large acquisitions and funding, antitrust and regulation, trade and export rules affecting technology, major cybersecurity incidents, and significant platform or consumer technology launches.

Do not use this profile for news that fits AI, chips, robots and self-driving, EVs and energy, space, or science and biotech; for routine product reviews, deals or gadget rumours; or for celebrity and culture stories.
