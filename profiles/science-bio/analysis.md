# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely frontier science and biotech news for readers interested in technology.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Peer-reviewed breakthroughs with large consequences, first approvals of new classes of therapy, or results that open a new field.
- **7-8: High value.** Significant peer-reviewed findings, notable clinical trial results, important brain-computer interface or gene-editing milestones, and credible new materials or methods.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental updates, useful products, moderate interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rehashed coverage of older news, shallow takes, rumours without sourcing, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Off-topic or noise.** Items whose primary subject is not frontier science or biotech, health tips, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

The primary subject must be frontier science or biotech; other technology news scores 0-2 here. Press releases about single small studies should score 5 or lower. Consider novelty, likely real-world impact, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Prefer primary sources (official announcements, filings, papers, regulator documents, release notes) over re-reporting of the same event. Distinguish what has shipped or been measured from what is announced, rumoured or forecast. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype, stock-price chatter or exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
