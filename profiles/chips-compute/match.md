# Chips & Computing profile

Use this profile for news whose primary subject is computing hardware and infrastructure: semiconductors and chip design, foundries and fabs (TSMC, Intel, Samsung), GPUs and AI accelerators (Nvidia, AMD and others), memory, data centers and their power needs, supercomputers, quantum computing, and export controls or industrial policy on chips.

Do not use this profile for consumer gadget reviews, deals, routine PC component launches without broader significance, or AI model news where hardware is only mentioned in passing.
