# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news about chips, AI compute, data centers and quantum computing for readers who follow where computing capacity is heading.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** New process nodes or architectures that change the industry, landmark export-control or industrial-policy decisions, major quantum computing milestones, or deals that reshape the supply of AI compute.
- **7-8: High value.** Significant chip or accelerator launches, fab investments, notable earnings guidance tied to AI demand, credible benchmarks, data-center power and capacity deals, or insightful supply-chain analysis.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental updates, useful products, moderate interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rehashed coverage of older news, shallow takes, rumours without sourcing, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Off-topic or noise.** Items whose primary subject is not computing hardware or infrastructure, consumer deals, routine reviews, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

The primary subject must be computing hardware, chips, data centers or quantum computing; other technology news scores 0-2 here. Consider novelty, likely real-world impact, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Prefer primary sources (official announcements, filings, papers, regulator documents, release notes) over re-reporting of the same event. Distinguish what has shipped or been measured from what is announced, rumoured or forecast. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype, stock-price chatter or exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
