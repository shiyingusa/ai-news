<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Google DeepMind has made Genie 4 available inside Project Genie for Google AI Ultra subscribers, calling it its most realistic world model to date. It generates interactive 3D worlds that can be edited in real time through text input, and for the first time includes native sound. The update also adds chainable worlds, a Project Genie agent that builds worlds, and a community gallery where users can share and remix what they create. Access is limited to "eligible" AI Ultra subscribers, and neither Google's post nor the coverage spells out which countries or account types qualify.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Google's own announcement states that Genie 4, a Project Genie agent, chainable worlds and a community gallery are rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers, and The Decoder reports that Genie 4 can be modified in real time via text and adds native sound for the first time.
- **Not yet confirmed:** the "most realistic world model to date" claim rests on Google's description with no independent benchmark or hands-on test, and the announcement leaves open the price of access beyond the Ultra subscription, the full list of eligible countries, and whether Workspace accounts can subscribe — a commenter reports their UK account still shows Genie 3.

</div>

## Background

Genie is Google DeepMind's line of "world models": systems that generate an interactive 3D environment on the fly from a prompt, frame by frame, instead of using pre-built game assets — so the scene can keep responding as a user moves through it. Genie 4 arrives as an update to Project Genie rather than a new product, and the same rollout adds features to Genie 3, the previous generation, which is still what some Google AI Ultra subscribers report seeing in their accounts. Access is tied to that subscription tier, and commenters note that prior Genie versions have been limited by region and account type.

## Why it matters

For anyone planning to build on Project Genie, the practical gate is the subscription and account type, not the model: check that your region and Google account are eligible for AI Ultra before designing around Genie 4, since a UK commenter reports their Ultra account still advertises Genie 3 and a Workspace account could not obtain Ultra at all.

## What to watch

Watch whether Google updates its Project Genie eligibility documentation to add countries beyond the current list and to state Workspace support, which would confirm or refute the access complaints raised on Hacker News.

## Community discussion

Hacker News commenters focused less on capability than on access: one said they wanted to pay Google but could not tell whether their Workspace account, their UK location, or a missing page update was blocking AI Ultra with Genie 4. Others debated the technology itself — questioning whether a generated world stays consistent when you spin 360°, praising DeepMind for work that reads as craft rather than profit-seeking, and speculating about generated worlds eventually serving as a game engine.
