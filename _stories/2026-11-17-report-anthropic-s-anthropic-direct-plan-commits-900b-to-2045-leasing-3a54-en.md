<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Anthropic is reportedly planning a data-center and compute program called Anthropic Direct that would commit more than $900 billion through 2045, according to Financial Times reporting based on internal documents and summarized by Techmeme. Those documents indicate the company would lease 875,000 TPUs — tensor processing units, the AI chips Google designs — for $7.2 billion a year. The report is set against a 600,000 sq ft data center under construction just inland from the Mississippi River in Louisiana, where about 1,300 workers are racing to finish the building. Neither Anthropic nor Google has confirmed the program's name, financial terms, or schedule, and the details come from a single outlet's account of documents it says it reviewed.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** The Financial Times reported, citing documents it reviewed, that Anthropic's internal data-centre programme is called Anthropic Direct and involves commitments of more than $900 billion through 2045, including a lease of 875,000 TPUs at $7.2 billion a year; the FT piece also describes a 600,000 sq ft Louisiana data centre with roughly 1,300 workers finishing construction.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Anthropic has not publicly confirmed the Anthropic Direct name, the $900B+ total, the 875K TPU count or the $7.2B annual figure, and the underlying documents are not public, so the TPU supplier, contract terms and payment schedule are unknown; the $900B+ figure also differs from the $807 billion Anthropic's October IPO filing said it planned to spend on AI infrastructure, and the sources do not reconcile the two numbers.

</div>

## Background

Anthropic's compute spending has been building toward this for months: AI Daily Radar's 15 November digest reported CEO Dario Amodei describing compute purchasing as the company's "deepest business dilemma" days before a planned record IPO, with commitments reported at nearly $901 billion and the listing framed partly as a financial buffer against costs future revenue might not justify. The FT report adds structure to that total — a named program, a horizon running to 2045, and per-year lease terms rather than a single aggregate figure. TPUs (tensor processing units) are Google's custom chips built for machine-learning workloads, an alternative to Nvidia's GPUs.

## Why it matters

If the reported terms hold, Anthropic would become one of the largest single lessees of TPU capacity, which matters to any team that depends on Google Cloud TPUs for its own training runs: multi-year commitments at that scale can tighten on-demand allocation and pricing for everyone else. For now the practical takeaway is to treat the numbers as unconfirmed until Anthropic or Google publishes contract or capacity details.

## What to watch

Watch for confirmation or denial from Anthropic or Google about the Anthropic Direct name and the $7.2 billion annual TPU lease, and for completion of the Louisiana data center the report describes, which would be the first physical evidence the buildout is proceeding on these terms.
