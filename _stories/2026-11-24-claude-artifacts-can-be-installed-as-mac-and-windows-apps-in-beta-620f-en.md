<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Anthropic is rolling out a beta feature called “Install as app” that turns Claude artifacts—interactive outputs generated in Claude chats, such as small apps or visualizations—into desktop apps on Mac and Windows. The installed artifacts can show notifications and, if the user enables it, get full computer access.

The available report does not specify which artifact types are supported, how many users can try the beta, or what safeguards govern the computer-access option.

</div>

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Anthropic’s Claude article, as summarized by Techmeme, says the beta “Install as app” feature lets users open Claude artifacts as Mac and Windows apps with notifications and optional full computer access.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Which artifact types can be installed, whether the beta is open to all Claude users or a limited group, and what “full computer access” permits or how it is gated; no independent test is available.

</div>

## Background

Claude artifacts are the interactive outputs Claude generates — small apps, tools and pages — that until now lived inside the Claude interface rather than on the desktop. Anthropic's own documentation says the new beta lets users "open the artifacts you use every day like any other app" in Claude for Mac and Windows, and Unite.AI reports the installed apps get their own windows and icons and can request access to files, programs, notifications and other operating-system features. The beta is rolling out to both free and paid plans, according to Unite.AI.

## Why it matters

For Claude users, an artifact can now behave like an ordinary desktop app, with notifications and, if opted in, access beyond the chat to the computer. The practical step is to review what an installed artifact can reach before granting full computer access.
