<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Uber confirmed that hackers stole driver's license information for close to 150,000 drivers in a security incident involving data stored by AI cloud company Fireworks AI. The stolen records are identity documents, and the report identifies Fireworks AI as the holder of the affected data; it does not specify how the attackers gained access, which jurisdictions are affected, or what notification Uber is providing.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Uber confirmed that personal information and driver's license data belonging to drivers was stolen in an October breach at a third-party technology provider, AI cloud company Fireworks AI, and TechCrunch reports the company is notifying close to 150,000 affected drivers (Uber, as reported by TechCrunch and Cybernews).
- **Not yet confirmed:** Exactly how attackers gained access rests on a single report, TechTimes, which said they used stolen internal credentials at Fireworks AI; Fireworks AI's own account of the incident, the precise number of drivers affected, whether additional data categories were taken, and any regulatory notification have not been established by the sources reviewed.

</div>

## Background

Fireworks AI, the vendor named in the breach, sells training and inference capacity for AI models, including a "virtual cloud" that spans 18+ regions across 8 providers, so customers can run models without operating their own GPU hardware. That business model means the company processes and stores data on behalf of its customers rather than only on their own systems, which is why driver's license records held there are part of Uber's exposure.

## Why it matters

For the roughly 150,000 drivers whose license data was exposed, the theft creates a durable identity-theft risk because driver's license numbers and images are often used for verification; affected drivers should watch for phishing or fraudulent account attempts. The source does not say whether Uber is offering credit monitoring or individual notices.
