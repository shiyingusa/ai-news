Tesla has raised the price of the Cybertruck's top Cyberbeast trim to $114,990 and stopped offering Cybertruck leases, according to Not a Tesla App. The report says delivery estimates for the Cyberbeast now stretch to June 2027.

It does not give the previous price, so the size of this latest increase — the report frames it as another rise — cannot be established from the available information. Tesla has not publicly explained either the price change or the end of leasing.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Not a Tesla App reported on November 25 that Tesla raised the Cyberbeast to $114,990 and ended Cybertruck leasing, with estimated deliveries stretching to June 2027; Teslahubs, citing the same change, puts the increase at $5,000 from $109,990 and gives a delivery window of March–June 2027, and KBB reported on November 17 that the Cyberbeast was the only Cybertruck trim still open for orders in the U.S.
- **Not yet confirmed:** No Tesla statement is quoted, so the company's own confirmation of the price, the scope of the lease removal (all trims and markets, or U.S. only) and the delivery dates rests on secondary reporting. The sources also disagree on the price figure: KBB listed $112,235 on November 17, before the reported $5,000 increase, while Teslahubs cites $109,990 as the prior price; the reports do not explain whether the gap reflects fees, destination charges or a different configuration.

</div>

## Background

The Cyberbeast is the highest-priced trim in Tesla's Cybertruck lineup, so its sticker price sets the ceiling for the model's pricing ladder. The "again" in the report indicates this is a repeat increase rather than a one-off adjustment, and ending leasing removes a path to the truck that did not require paying the full purchase price up front. The delivery window now stretching to June 2027 suggests orders are outpacing current production, though the report does not give a reason for either the price move or the leasing decision.

## Why it matters

Prospective buyers lose the lease route entirely, so taking delivery now means financing the full $114,990 purchase price rather than a lower monthly lease payment, and there is no lease-end option to return the truck. Buyers should confirm the price shown on Tesla's configurator at the time they order, because the report does not say whether existing orders or reservations are protected at an earlier price.

## What to watch

Watch Tesla's order page for the Cyberbeast's delivery estimate, which the report puts at June 2027, and for whether a lease option reappears on any Cybertruck trim.
