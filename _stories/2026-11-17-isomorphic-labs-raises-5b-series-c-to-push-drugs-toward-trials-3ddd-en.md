Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google's DeepMind and now part of Alphabet, raised $5 billion in a Series C round, six months after a $2.1 billion round. The company, led by Demis Hassabis, plans to use the capital to push its drug pipeline toward clinical trials; it has no approved drugs yet. ## Background

Isomorphic Labs was spun out of Google's DeepMind in 2021 and is part of Alphabet; Demis Hassabis leads the company. AI drug discovery uses machine-learning models to propose and prioritize molecules that still require laboratory and clinical validation, a long and expensive process.

## Why it matters

The round gives Isomorphic a larger financial runway to fund preclinical work and early clinical testing, but because no candidate or trial date has been named, the move into human trials remains a goal rather than a scheduled step.

## What to watch

Watch for Isomorphic to name a specific drug candidate and disclose an investigational new drug (IND) filing or a Phase 1 trial start, which would show the pipeline has moved from computational work into clinical testing.
