## Background

Denza is BYD's premium and luxury brand, and its Indian entry follows the start of its European operations earlier this year, according to electrive. Autocar India dates the Indian launch to 13 November 2026 and describes the D9 as a seven-seat luxury MPV, with the Z9 GT launched alongside it as the second model.

## Why it matters

For Indian premium EV buyers, Denza's entry adds the D9 and Z9 GT as options, but the missing price and local-production details mean affordability and delivery timing cannot yet be assessed.
