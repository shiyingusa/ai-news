<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

US curbs on chip access, measures against distilling US models' outputs, and shrinking access to Nvidia hardware abroad are slowing China's leading AI labs, according to an unnamed report cited by The Decoder, with DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Alibaba named as the most pressured. DeepSeek researchers estimate internally that they are up to nine months behind US models and that the gap is widening, the report says. The account names no specific models, benchmarks or measurement date behind the nine-month figure, and offers no documentation from the companies, chip suppliers or US regulators.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** The Decoder reported on 25 November 2026 that US chip-access curbs, anti-distillation measures and reduced access to Nvidia hardware abroad are pressuring DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Alibaba, and that DeepSeek researchers have internally estimated a gap of up to nine months behind US models — claims the outlet attributes to a report it does not name.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The nine-month figure, the assertion that the gap is widening, and the causal link to export controls and anti-distillation rules all rest on that single unnamed report; no methodology, measurement basis or independent benchmark is given, none of the three labs has commented on the record, and the report is not identified so it cannot be checked.

</div>

## Background

US export controls had already been widened before this report: AI Daily Radar's November 16 digest covered a Commerce Department interim rule requiring licenses for AI chips when the hardware may supply compute to China or other restricted countries, extending controls beyond physical shipments to remote access. On November 24 the same digest series noted the department added Apex Enterprise Solutions, a Singapore-based Alibaba subsidiary, to the Entity List — a roster requiring US suppliers to obtain a license before exporting controlled goods, including advanced AI chips. "Distillation" in this context means training a model on the outputs of a stronger one, a route labs have used to narrow a capability gap without matching compute.

## Why it matters

Teams building on Chinese open-weight releases from these labs face two practical effects: a less predictable release cadence and, if the internal estimate holds, a frontier gap of roughly three quarters rather than months — so a model shipped today would be benchmarked against US systems that are already several generations ahead.

## What to watch

Watch DeepSeek's next flagship model release and independent benchmark results on it, which would be the first public test of the nine-month internal estimate. Also watch whether the underlying report is published or corroborated by another outlet, since no primary documentation has surfaced so far.
