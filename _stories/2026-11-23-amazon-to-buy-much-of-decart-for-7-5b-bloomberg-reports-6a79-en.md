<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Amazon has agreed to acquire much of AI startup Decart, including its Decart Optimization Stack software along with its founding team and AI researchers, for roughly $7.5 billion, Bloomberg reported on November 23, citing one unnamed source. The deal has not been publicly confirmed by either company in the reporting, so the price, the precise assets that transfer, and the timing all remain unverified. TechCrunch framed the purchase as part of Amazon's push to strengthen the AI infrastructure underpinning its in-house Trainium chips as it prepares to sell that silicon to outside customers.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Amazon has agreed to acquire much of the AI startup Decart, including its Decart Optimization Stack along with its founding team and AI researchers, Bloomberg's Matt Day reported, citing a person familiar with the matter; Amazon's own announcement confirmed the agreement but gave no price, according to explainx.ai's account of that post. The deal is described as signed, not closed.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The ~$7.5 billion figure comes only from Bloomberg's unnamed source — Amazon has not disclosed a price, and the same reporting says Amazon is not acquiring Decart's AI models (Lucy, Oasis and similar). What exactly is excluded, the deal's closing conditions and timing, and any link to Trainium sales plans are not established by the primary sources.

</div>

## 背景

Decart 于 2023 年由 Dean Leitersdorf 和 Moshe Shalev 创立，于 2024 年 10 月公开启动，并完成由 Oren Zeev 领投的 2100 万美元种子轮融资；到 2026 年 5 月，它表示已从包括 NVIDIA、Adobe Ventures、Toyota Ventures、Sequoia 和 Benchmark 在内的投资方处筹集了 3 亿美元 C 轮融资。其公开产品包括世界模型 Lucy、Oasis 以及被收购的 Decart Optimization Stack（DOS）。

## Why it matters

Decart's Optimization Stack sits between models and chips, and the company says it runs across Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs and Amazon Trainium — claiming 1,600 tokens per second for agentic inference against an industry average it puts near 200. Buying it would give AWS ownership of the software layer it needs to pitch Trainium on cost per token rather than raw hardware, so teams weighing Trainium against rival accelerators should watch one open compatibility question: whether the stack stays available on Nvidia and Google silicon or becomes AWS-exclusive, which Bloomberg's report does not address.

## What to watch

Watch for public confirmation from Amazon or Decart, or disclosure in a regulatory filing, which would establish the $7.5 billion figure and the scope of the transferred technology and staff. Any detail on how the Optimization Stack feeds Amazon's Trainium roadmap would show whether the chip-selling rationale holds.
