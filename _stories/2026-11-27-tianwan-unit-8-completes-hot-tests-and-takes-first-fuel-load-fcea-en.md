<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Tianwan unit 8 in China has completed hot tests — the pre-operational run-through of plant systems before the reactor is started — and received its initial fuel load, World Nuclear News reported. Delivering fuel moves the unit into the last stage of commissioning, ahead of start-up and connection to the grid.

The site is expected to become the world's largest nuclear power plant once all eight of its units are in operation, a status that depends on every unit running rather than on unit 8's fuel delivery alone.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** World Nuclear News reports that hot tests are complete for Tianwan unit 8 and that its initial fuel load has been delivered.
- **Not yet confirmed:** No start-up date, grid-connection date or commercial operation date is given, and no independent confirmation of the testing results is available; the "world's largest nuclear power plant" description rests on the site's expectation that all eight units will eventually operate.

</div>

## Background

Tianwan sits on the Yellow Sea coast near Lianyungang in Jiangsu province, and its newest pair, units 7 and 8, are Russian-designed VVER-1200 pressurized water reactors built under agreements Russia and China signed in June 2018; units 5 and 6 use Chinese ACPR1000 reactors. Hot tests are the non-nuclear checks of plant systems at operating temperature and pressure, and delivery of the initial fuel load is a later commissioning step before a reactor can start. Unit 7, the first of the VVER-1200 pair, had already begun loading fuel in a reported shift from construction to nuclear commissioning.

## Why it matters

Fuel loading is the point after which a reactor's schedule is measured in start-up steps rather than construction milestones, so unit 8 is now the unit to track for new baseload capacity at Tianwan. The site cannot claim the "world's largest" title until all eight units are running, which makes the operating status of the other units as important as unit 8's progress.

## What to watch

Watch for the announcement of first criticality and grid connection for Tianwan unit 8, the next checkable steps before commercial operation.
