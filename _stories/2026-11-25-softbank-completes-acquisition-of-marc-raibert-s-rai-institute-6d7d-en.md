SoftBank has completed its acquisition of the Robotics and AI Institute (RAI) from Hyundai Motor Group, The Robot Report reported, with financial terms undisclosed; SoftBank answered questions about the deal but declined to comment. The Cambridge, Massachusetts-based institute, founded by Boston Dynamics founder Marc Raibert in 2022 with more than $400 million in initial funding from Hyundai, works on mobility, manipulation, perception and intelligence, and currently lists 24 open positions across Cambridge and Zurich.

The Robot Report first reported the acquisition agreement in September 2026, and it remains unstated whether SoftBank ownership will change the institute's research priorities or whether RAI will keep operating independently.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** The Robot Report reported that SoftBank has completed its acquisition of the Robotics and AI Institute from Hyundai Motor Group, following the outlet's September 2026 report that the two sides had agreed to a deal; the institute was founded in 2022 by Boston Dynamics founder Marc Raibert with more than $400 million in initial funding from Hyundai.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The purchase price and any other financial terms, which were not disclosed; SoftBank declined to comment to The Robot Report, so completion rests on the outlet's reporting rather than a company announcement; and it remains unclear whether RAI will keep operating independently or whether SoftBank's ownership will shift its research priorities.

</div>

## Background

SoftBank is not new to Marc Raibert's robots: it bought Boston Dynamics from Alphabet in 2017, then sold a controlling stake to Hyundai in 2021 in a deal valuing the company at $1.1 billion, and Hyundai has since bought out SoftBank's remaining 9.65% stake — ending a nearly decade-long investment. Raibert founded the Cambridge, Massachusetts-based Robotics and AI Institute in 2022 with more than $400 million in initial funding from Hyundai to work on mobility, manipulation, perception and intelligence. SoftBank has been steadily assembling robotics holdings — AI Daily Radar's November 16 digest reported that it led a $500 million Series F in Agile Robots at an $8.5 billion valuation — and the institute remains a research organization with 24 open positions across Cambridge and Zurich.

## Why it matters

RAI is hiring into an ownership change: its 24 open roles in Cambridge and Zurich, spanning robot learning, manipulation, simulation, navigation and hardware engineering, sit under a new owner that has not said whether the institute's research agenda stays the same. The deal also places RAI alongside SoftBank portfolio companies including Skild AI, Agile Robots and 1X Technologies, overlap The Robot Report flags as a possible source of collaboration but not a stated plan.

## What to watch

Whether SoftBank discloses deal terms or an integration plan for RAI, which it declined to discuss. Separately, the fate of SoftBank's planned $5.375 billion acquisition of ABB Robotics remains open after Chinese regulators blocked that deal.
