TechCrunch reports that after Anthropic's IPO, Amazon will hold just under 15% of the AI lab and Google nearly 14%, giving the two cloud companies close to 30% combined. Amazon also has an option to increase its stake substantially, though the report does not give the terms or timing. The figures describe post-IPO ownership, and the supplied article does not provide an IPO date, valuation, or confirmation from Anthropic, Amazon, or Google.

## Background

Anthropic's IPO has been in the works for months: AI Daily Radar's Nov. 15 digest reported that days before a planned record listing, CEO Dario Amodei called buying compute the company's "deepest business dilemma," with commitments totaling nearly $901 billion and the offering framed largely as a financial buffer against revenue that may not cover those costs. The two stakes in question are conversions of pre-IPO holdings rather than new purchases — Amazon's roughly 14.7% arrives through promissory note conversions that could lift it to as much as 23.8% if all notes convert, while Google's 13.7% comes via non-voting shares and a private placement.

## Why it matters

The combined stake would give Amazon and Google formal influence over Anthropic's governance after the IPO, with Amazon's option potentially giving it a larger bloc than Google's. For organizations building on Anthropic's models, that ownership balance is the relevant signal; the report discloses no change to model access, pricing, or cloud terms.

## What to watch

Watch Anthropic's IPO filing and final prospectus for the confirmed ownership percentages and the terms of Amazon's option to increase its stake.
