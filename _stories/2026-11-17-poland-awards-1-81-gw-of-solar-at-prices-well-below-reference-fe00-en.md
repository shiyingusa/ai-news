Poland's Energy Regulatory Office (URE) awarded about 1.81 GW of solar capacity in two renewable energy auctions held on Nov. 9 and 10, with photovoltaic projects accounting for 65 of the 68 successful bids. In the main category for projects above 1 MW, URE selected 50 bids from 22 developers covering roughly 1,799 MW of solar and 64 MW of onshore wind, at solar prices of PLN 180 ($49)/MWh to PLN 327.77/MWh against a PLN 389/MWh reference price; that auction contracted about 17.7 TWh of electricity worth more than PLN 5.5 billion ($1.5 billion), only around 33% of the volume on offer.

The separate auction for installations up to 1 MW selected 18 solar bids from 13 developers totalling about 15.6 MW, at PLN 320/MWh to PLN 377.77/MWh against a PLN 414/MWh reference, contracting just under 0.15 TWh, or 1.3% of available volume. Auctions for hydropower, agricultural biogas, and biomass or non-agricultural biogas installations again produced no successful bids.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** URE's published results, as reported by pv magazine, show 68 winning bids across the two November auctions, 65 of them solar, awarding about 1,814 MW of PV (1,799 MW in the above-1 MW category plus 15.6 MW in the sub-1 MW category), with the clearing prices, contracted volumes and PLN 5.5 billion total value given above.
- **Not yet confirmed:** These are awarded, contracted capacities, not built plants — the article gives no commissioning dates or project-level detail, and the dollar figures are the publication's own conversions. It also does not explain whether the 1.3% subscription in the small-installation auction reflects developer disinterest or a small volume offered.

</div>

## Background

Poland's renewable auctions sit inside a support framework for 2022–27 that originally targeted about 9 GW of new solar and 3 GW of onshore wind, with developers competing on price against a regulator-set reference level. In the 2025 round, URE allocated roughly 1.67 GW of solar and 82.7 MW of wind, with PV taking 178 of 181 successful bids and winning solar prices of PLN 216.90/MWh to PLN 374.77/MWh — so this year's round is somewhat larger and its cheapest bids clear lower. Poland installed about 3.6 GW of solar in 2025, bringing cumulative capacity to around 24.8 GW, according to figures compiled by Agencja Rynku Energii for the Ministry of Energy.

## Why it matters

The cheapest winning solar bid of PLN 180/MWh is about 17% below the PLN 216.90/MWh floor of the 2025 round and roughly 54% below this year's PLN 389/MWh reference price, so developers who won have locked in materially lower revenue per megawatt-hour than a year ago. Because only about a third of the offered large-project volume was contracted and 1.3% of the small-installation volume, most of the 2022–27 support envelope remains unallocated — developers and investors should watch how URE re-offers that capacity rather than assume this round's price levels carry over.

## What to watch

Watch whether URE re-offers the uncontracted volume — roughly two-thirds of the large-project pot and almost all of the sub-1 MW pot — in a subsequent round. Also track Poland's cumulative PV against the framework's original ~9 GW solar target, after the country reached about 24.8 GW at the end of 2025.
