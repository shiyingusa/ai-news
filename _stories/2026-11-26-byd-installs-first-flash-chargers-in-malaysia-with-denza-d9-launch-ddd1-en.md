<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

BYD has installed its first Flash Chargers in Malaysia, timing the deployment with the launch of the Denza D9 equipped with its second-generation Blade battery. The chargers are described as megawatt-class, and BYD says access will soon be extended to all EV brands.

The report did not specify how many chargers were installed, where they are located, when open access will begin, or what charging rates have been independently measured.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** BYD Malaysia has switched on its Flash Charging network, launched alongside the 2027 Denza D9; Malaysian outlets report chargers are live at three Denza showrooms (Balakong, Glenmarie and Old Klang Road), with 1,500 kW output available to vehicles using the second-generation Blade battery, and that charging will be free for Denza D9 and Z9GT owners until end-2027 (paultan.org, autobuzz.my, dsf.my).
- **Not yet confirmed:** The headline performance figures — 10–70% in five minutes and roughly 400 km of added range — are BYD's own claims at launch, not independent test results; the plan for 12 locations this year and 130 by 2027 comes from the same launch coverage, and the promise of access for other EV brands has no date, pricing or connector/power conditions attached.

</div>

## Background

BYD's Flash Charging is the company's megawatt-class charging architecture: overhead-cable posts rated at up to 1,500 kW, which BYD says can take vehicles fitted with its Blade 2.0 battery to nearly full in under 10 minutes. TechCrunch reported in March 2026 that BYD had begun rolling out EV batteries advertising 5-minute "flash charging."

## Why it matters

For Malaysian buyers of the Denza D9, the launch pairs the car with a charging network operated by its manufacturer. Drivers of other EV brands should treat the promised access as a plan rather than an available service until BYD publishes a start date and charger locations.
