<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Meta and Amazon are in ongoing discussions about a deal that would connect Meta's Muse AI agent to Amazon and end Amazon's block on that connection, Fortune reported, citing unnamed sources. Any agreement would require sign-off from both companies' CEOs.

The report did not disclose proposed terms, timing, or the scope of what Muse would be allowed to do on Amazon, and neither company has publicly confirmed the talks.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Fortune, citing unnamed sources, reported that Meta and Amazon are discussing a deal to connect Muse to Amazon and end Amazon's block, and that any deal would need both CEOs' approval.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Whether the discussions will produce a deal, its terms, timing or scope, and why Amazon blocked Muse; neither Meta nor Amazon has publicly confirmed the talks.

</div>

## Background

AI agents are software that can carry out tasks on a user's behalf; connecting one to a company's platform would let it operate inside that company's systems. Fortune's report does not explain how Muse would connect to Amazon or what Amazon's block currently prevents.

## Why it matters

If completed, the deal would give Meta a way to run Muse inside Amazon and give Amazon a say over how a third-party AI agent operates on its platform; until then, the block remains in place. The CEO-approval requirement means no access should be expected until a deal is publicly confirmed.
