<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Toyota has confirmed that the Highlander 9X will go on sale in early 2027 as the brand's first extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) — a plug-in vehicle whose combustion engine acts mainly as a generator to recharge the battery rather than drive the wheels. The model will use technology from Huawei and CATL, according to Electrek.

Toyota has not disclosed the vehicle's electric range, battery size, powertrain output, price, or which markets it will be sold in, so the early-2027 date is the only firm detail beyond the two Chinese suppliers named.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Toyota confirmed the Highlander 9X as its first EREV with an early-2027 on-sale date and named Huawei and CATL as technology suppliers, Electrek reported.
- **Not yet confirmed:** range, battery capacity, power output, price, target markets, and exactly which components Huawei and CATL will supply; no independent testing or production-model specifications exist yet.

</div>

## Background

An extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) drives its wheels with an electric motor and carries a small gasoline engine that works only as a generator to recharge the battery, so it runs on electricity most of the time but refuels instead of relying on a charger. The Highlander 9X has been signposted since GAC Toyota's June 2025 technology day, where the joint venture said Chinese engineers would lead development of the next-generation Highlander and Sienna for the Chinese market and that both would get range-extended powertrains, according to CarNewsChina.

## Why it matters

For a company that built its electrified lineup around its own hybrid systems, Toyota's first EREV will depend on Huawei and CATL for core electrified technology — so the model's specifications and timing rest on those supplier relationships. Buyers cross-shopping it against Toyota's existing hybrids and plug-in hybrids have no range or price figures to compare until Toyota publishes them.

## What to watch

Watch for Toyota to release the Highlander 9X's powertrain and battery specifications and to confirm which markets it will be sold in, ahead of the stated early-2027 on-sale date.
