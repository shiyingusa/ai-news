<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Noeri, a five-month-old startup co-founded by two Stanford AI experts, has raised $110 million in a funding round led by Menlo Ventures, Bloomberg reported. Noeri is developing software that reviews clients' AI models and tools and suggests improvements. The report did not disclose the company's valuation, customers, product benchmarks, pricing, or launch timing.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Bloomberg's report says Noeri, a five-month-old startup, raised $110M led by Menlo Ventures for software that reviews clients' AI models and tools; separate reports corroborate the round and identify Stanford professors Carlos Guestrin and Yejin Choi as co-founders, with Nvidia, Snowflake, Cisco, Madrona, Kindred Ventures and NEA also investing.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Noeri's claimed performance and cost improvements, named customers, independent benchmarks, pricing, and availability; the Bloomberg excerpt itself names neither the founders nor the additional investors.

</div>

## Background

Menlo Ventures, which led the round, publishes a State of Enterprise AI survey; its fourth annual edition, fielded among nearly 400 U.S. enterprise decision-makers in September 2026, estimates enterprise AI spending at $177 billion, up from $33 billion in 2025. The same survey reports that agents now drive 80% of enterprise AI usage.

## Why it matters

For teams choosing AI evaluation and model-improvement tools, Noeri adds a well-funded but unproven option; because no release date, pricing, or customer deployments were disclosed, it cannot yet be compared with existing products.
