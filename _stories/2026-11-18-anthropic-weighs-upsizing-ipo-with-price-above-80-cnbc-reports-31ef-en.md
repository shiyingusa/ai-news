<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Anthropic is considering enlarging its initial public offering and pricing shares well above $80, according to CNBC, which cited people familiar with the matter. That would be above the $70–$78 per share range previously indicated for the offering, with the report attributing the move to surging investor demand. No final price, share count, total proceeds, valuation or pricing date was reported, and the figures rest on unnamed sources rather than an Anthropic announcement or a filed pricing notice.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** CNBC reported, citing people familiar with the details, that Anthropic is weighing an upsized IPO priced well above $80 a share, versus a prior $70–$78 range.
- **Not yet confirmed:** that the deal will actually be upsized, the final price, the number of shares, total proceeds, the implied valuation, the pricing date, and any comment from Anthropic itself — all of these rest on unnamed sources.

</div>

## Background

Anthropic's updated SEC filing on November 10 set a range of $70–$78 per share for 825 million shares in the offering, as CNBC and Yahoo Finance reported at the time, and by November 17 CNBC was already reporting that the stock was likely to price above that range. AI Daily Radar's November 15 digest reported CEO Dario Amodei calling compute purchases the company's "deepest business dilemma," with commitments near $901 billion and the IPO framed as a financial buffer in case future revenue does not justify those costs. The latest reporting continues that sequence: instead of trimming the deal, Anthropic is said to be weighing selling more shares at a higher price.

## Why it matters

If the deal prices above $80, investors who indicated interest at $70–$78 would pay more per share, and Anthropic would raise more gross proceeds at a higher valuation — the figure that will then anchor its share-based compensation and serve as the reference point for how public markets price other large AI labs.

## What to watch

Watch for Anthropic's official IPO pricing announcement, which would disclose the final price, share count, proceeds and valuation, and confirm or contradict the reported upsizing.
