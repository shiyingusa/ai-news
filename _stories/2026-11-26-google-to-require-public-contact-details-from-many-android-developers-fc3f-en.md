<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Google has announced that, starting next year, many independent Android developers will have to publish a phone number and address publicly on Google Play. The change is meant to bring the store in line with the EU's Digital Services Act (DSA), a law that requires online platforms to identify the businesses that sell on them.

Developers have until February 1st, 2027 to declare whether they qualify as a "trader" under UK and European law — broadly, whether they earn revenue from their apps, especially regularly or at high volume. Google says the contact details will be shown to users worldwide, not just in the EU.

The Verge's report is based on Google's developer support documentation and does not include the full list of exemptions, which the excerpt cuts off.

</div>

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Google’s Play Console help page says every active developer account must declare whether it is a “trader” or “non-trader” under EEA and UK law by February 1, 2027, regardless of where its apps are distributed; The Verge reported that Google announced the change to comply with the EU Digital Services Act and that contact details will be made public worldwide.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Which developers will be classified as traders and exactly which phone number and address will be published; Google’s revenue-based guidance is broad, the report is truncated, and no independent confirmation or implementation details are available.

</div>

## Background

Google says the public contact requirement is part of complying with the EU's Digital Services Act, which sets rules for online platforms. The policy turns on whether a developer qualifies as a "trader" under UK and European law: Google broadly defines that as earning revenue from apps, especially regularly or at high volume, and says apps outside that definition are exempt from the declaration.

## Why it matters

For developers who earn money from their apps, the decision is effectively binary and global: declaring trader status means a phone number and address — potentially a home address for solo developers — become visible to every Play Store user, while the alternative is to claim an exemption they may not qualify for. Developers outside Europe should not assume they are unaffected, since Google says it will display the details worldwide.

## What to watch

The February 1st, 2027 deadline for developers to declare trader status, and how Google handles accounts that decline to declare or are judged to have declared incorrectly.
