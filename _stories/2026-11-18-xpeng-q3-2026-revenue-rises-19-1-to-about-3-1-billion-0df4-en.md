Xpeng reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of approximately €3.1 billion, up 19.1% from the same period a year earlier, according to its quarterly results as reported by electrive. The company said the technology partnership with Volkswagen contributed to growth once again, alongside its own vehicle business. Xpeng remains loss-making for the quarter; the report does not give a net loss figure, delivery numbers or a timeline for reaching profitability.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Xpeng's Q3 2026 results, as reported by electrive, show revenue of about €3.1 billion (RMB 24.28 billion), up 19.1% year on year, with the company still posting a loss; cnevpost reports the figure came in above the top of Xpeng's own guidance, on 118,390 deliveries.
- **Not yet confirmed:** how much of the growth came from the Volkswagen technology partnership — the source says only that it "once again contributed", with no figure — and the details of the Q4 delivery guidance of 169,000–174,000 units, the 13.1% vehicle margin, a net loss that widened 127.9% year on year while narrowing 35.1% quarter on quarter, and a plan to at least double overseas sales in 2027, all of which rest on secondary reports of the earnings call rather than the source.

</div>

## Background

The 19.1% figure compares the quarter with the same period a year earlier, and the euro amount is a conversion of results Xpeng reports in its home currency. Revenue growth and profitability are separate measures here: the quarter brought in more money but still closed at a loss, which is why the report describes the company as remaining loss-making rather than turning profitable. Xpeng's revenue also includes technology-partnership income alongside vehicle sales, and the Volkswagen tie-up is the component the report credits with contributing to growth again this quarter.

## Why it matters

Because part of Xpeng's top-line growth comes from licensing technology to Volkswagen rather than from selling cars, the revenue figure is not a clean read on Chinese EV demand — readers tracking the market should separate the two, and watch whether the continued loss narrows as that partnership revenue scales.

## What to watch

Xpeng's fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results, which will show whether revenue from the Volkswagen partnership keeps growing and whether the company's losses narrow.
