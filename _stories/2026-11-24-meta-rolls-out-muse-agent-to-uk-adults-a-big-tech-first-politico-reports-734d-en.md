<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Meta has rolled out its personal AI agent Muse to adults in the UK, with Politico describing it as the first AI agent from a Big Tech company to launch in the country. The agent is pitched at helping with everyday tasks, but the report does not say which tasks it handles, what it costs, or which platforms it runs on. UK watchdogs have warned about AI agents, and Politico reports that regulators will be watching the Muse rollout closely.

- **Verified:** Meta rolled out its personal AI agent Muse to UK adults, as reported by Politico, which characterizes it as the first Big Tech AI agent launch in the UK.

</div>

## Background

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent: users chat with it in the Meta AI app or directly in WhatsApp, and it carries out tasks on their behalf — chasing a refund, renewing car insurance, turning saved travel Reels into an itinerary — rather than only answering questions. AI Daily Radar's 23 November digest reported that Walmart had made Muse the first shopping agent on its new Agent Gateway, able to search for and buy Black Friday deals through Walmart's Sparky assistant, with spending limits and purchase approvals set in a dedicated "Agent Profiles" space inside the customer's account.

## Why it matters

UK adults can now delegate everyday tasks to an agent built by Meta, at a point when UK watchdogs have already flagged concerns about agents generally. The terms Meta sets for what Muse can access and do will be the first worked example of how those warnings are applied to a Big Tech agent, and will shape the ground rules competitors face when they bring similar products to the UK.

## What to watch

Watch for the first public statement or action from UK regulators specifically about Muse, and for whether Meta names additional countries for the agent's rollout.
