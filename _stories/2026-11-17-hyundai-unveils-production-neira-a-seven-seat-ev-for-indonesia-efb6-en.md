<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Hyundai has shown the production version of the Neira, a three-row, seven-seat electric SUV, for the first time. The vehicle is scheduled to launch in Indonesia on November 20, according to Electrek. The report did not include battery size, range, power output, price or plans for markets outside Indonesia, so the Neira's specifications and positioning remain undisclosed.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Hyundai publicly revealed a production-ready Neira, described as a three-row, seven-seat electric SUV, with an Indonesia launch set for November 20, as reported by Electrek.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Range, battery capacity, charging speed, price and production volume; whether other markets will get the Neira; and whether the November 20 date marks the start of customer deliveries or only a market premiere, since the report does not specify.

</div>

## Background

Hyundai first showed the Neira in August 2026 as a prototype: a seven-seat electric SUV designed around Indonesian market requirements and targeted for local production with roughly 80% domestic content (TKDN, Indonesia's local-content threshold for vehicles). Indonesian outlets reported in early November that the model would make its debut in late November 2026, consistent with the November 20 launch date in the Electrek report. The new item is the first look at the production version rather than the prototype.

## What to watch

Hyundai's Indonesia launch event on November 20, where the company is expected to release the Neira's specifications and pricing.
