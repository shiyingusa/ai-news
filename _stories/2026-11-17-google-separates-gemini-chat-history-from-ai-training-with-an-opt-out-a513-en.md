Google is rolling out new Gemini app settings that split two things that were previously bundled: keeping your chat history and allowing your chats to be used for AI training. Until now, the only way to stop Google from training on your conversations and attachments was to turn off history entirely, which also meant losing the ability to return to a past chat. With the change, users get separate toggles, and the history setting keeps its adjustable retention limit. Training remains on by default — blocking it is an opt-out, found under Activity in Gemini settings, or via your Google account under Data and Privacy > My Activity > Other activity > Gemini Apps Activity.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Google's own blog post and Ars Technica both state that separate toggles for model training and chat history are rolling out in the Gemini app, that training is an opt-out setting located under Activity (or via My Activity > Other activity > Gemini Apps Activity), and that the history setting retains an adjustable limit.
- **Not yet confirmed:** the completion date or regional scope of the rollout, whether the same split applies to Google AI surfaces outside the Gemini app, and whether anything changes for conversations already collected — the sources describe only the new controls, not retroactive effects.

</div>

## Background

Until now, Gemini tied the two settings together: the only way to keep chats and attachments out of AI training was to turn off chat history entirely, which also meant losing the ability to return to an earlier conversation. The new rollout splits that into separate toggles, and training remains on by default — users have to actively switch it off under Activity in Gemini settings, or via their Google account at Data and Privacy > My Activity > Other activity > Gemini Apps Activity.

## Why it matters

Gemini users who want persistent chat history no longer have to trade it away to keep their conversations out of training data, which removes the main practical reason people disabled history. The catch is that the protection is not the default: anyone who wants it must go find the training toggle themselves, and chats remain eligible for training until they do.

## What to watch

Watch for the separate toggles to appear across accounts and regions, and for whether Google extends the same history-versus-training split to its other AI surfaces rather than only the Gemini app.
