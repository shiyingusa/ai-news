<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Google's AI Mode in Search can now draw on data from Google Drive and Google Wallet to personalize its answers, and the same Personal Intelligence capability is being extended to the standalone Gemini app, TechCrunch reported on November 23. The practical shift is that AI Mode responses can be grounded in a user's own files and saved items rather than only the public web, with the personalization running inside Google's existing ecosystem instead of through a third-party connector. The report did not say which countries or account types get the feature, whether users must opt in, or which Drive file types and Wallet contents can be read.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** TechCrunch reported that Personal Intelligence in AI Mode now reaches Drive and Wallet and is coming to the Gemini app, describing it as Google drawing on user data already inside its ecosystem.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Google's own announcement, the rollout scope (countries, account types, free versus paid tiers), whether the data linkage is opt-in or on by default, which file types in Drive are searchable, and any independent test of answer quality or privacy behavior — the report supplies none of these.

</div>

## Background

Personal Intelligence is Google's label for connecting data from apps people already use — Gmail, Photos, YouTube and Search — so Gemini and AI Mode can answer using personal context rather than general knowledge. Google said on November 13 that all Gemini app users in the U.S. could connect Google Wallet for responses informed by their spending habits; the new step carries that Wallet connection, plus Drive, into AI Mode in Search rather than leaving it inside the Gemini app.

## Why it matters

For people who keep documents in Drive and passes or cards in Wallet, an AI Mode answer can now reflect private material rather than only public web pages, so it is worth checking which Google apps are linked to AI Mode and whether that connection is opt-in before treating a response as purely web-sourced. The report does not state the default setting, so account-level review is the practical step.
