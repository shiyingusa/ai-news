<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Arriva has won a long-term contract to run bus services in the northwest of the Dutch province of North Holland, a deal valued at roughly €2 billion. The agreement covers the deployment of more than 200 electric buses alongside adjustments to the route network.

According to electrive, the award extends Arriva's recent run of major contract wins in the Netherlands. The report does not specify when the buses enter service, which manufacturer will supply them, or how much of the €2 billion is vehicle investment versus operating subsidy.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Arriva's own announcement states the North-West North Holland bus contract is worth about €2 billion and runs 13 years from July 2028, with a two-year option; the fleet will be 100% electric and number more than 200 buses, which Arriva calls its largest-ever bus contract (Arriva; also reported by Sustainable Bus and Bus News).
- **Not yet confirmed:** the split between new and existing vehicles, which manufacturers will supply the buses, and how much of the €2 billion covers vehicles versus operations are not specified; the initial electrive report gave no start date or operator-level detail, so those figures rest on Arriva's release and the trade press rather than an independent test or regulatory filing.

</div>

## Background

The deal is a concession: a long-term award by a regional transport authority to a single operator to run an area's bus network, which is why the electric fleet and the route changes are bundled into the same contract rather than procured separately. Arriva already runs bus services in the Netherlands, and the report describes this award as extending a run of major contract wins there.

## Why it matters

More than 200 electric buses have to be charged, serviced and parked, so the award obliges Arriva to build depot charging and maintenance capacity at a scale well beyond a pilot — in practice the main constraint on when the vehicles enter service. The vehicle order is also still open: the contract names no manufacturer, and Arriva has sourced e-buses from BYD before, ordering 82 of them for its Budapest zero-emission tender, so the bus and charging-equipment contracts are the concrete openings for suppliers.
