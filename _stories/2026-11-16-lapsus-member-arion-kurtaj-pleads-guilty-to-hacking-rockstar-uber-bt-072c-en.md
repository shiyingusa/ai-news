Arion Kurtaj, 21, a member of the Lapsus$ extortion group, pleaded guilty in a UK court to a string of online crimes, including attempting to extort Rockstar Games after stealing dozens of unfinished clips of Grand Theft Auto 6, according to a BBC report by Joe Tidy. The plea covers intrusions against Rockstar, Uber, BT and other organisations, and came after Kurtaj was released from a secure hospital.

The BBC account, relayed by Techmeme, does not specify the individual charges, the court, the hearing date or the sentence Kurtaj faces. The guilty pleas are a court outcome rather than a vendor claim, though the details rest on the BBC's reporting alone in the material supplied here.

## Background

Lapsus$ is the name given to a cybercrime group whose members have been accused of breaking into major technology and gaming companies. Arion Kurtaj's intrusions date to 2022, when he hacked Rockstar Games and leaked unfinished Grand Theft Auto 6 footage — including the first appearance of the character Lucia — and he has now pleaded guilty to that and other offences (BBC, IGN, Times Now). He had been held in a secure hospital and was assessed as fit to stand trial, which is why the case has reached a guilty plea only now (BBC, Times Now).

## Why it matters

The pleas turn the Rockstar extortion from an allegation into an admitted offence, so the remaining open question for the studio and the other targets is punishment rather than proof; game and technology companies that hold unreleased material now have a UK precedent in which leaking it was prosecuted as extortion.

## What to watch

The next milestone is Kurtaj's sentencing hearing; the BBC report gives no date, so watch for the court to set one and for the sentence to be published.
