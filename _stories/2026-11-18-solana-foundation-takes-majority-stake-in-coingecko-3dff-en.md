<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

The Solana Foundation has taken a majority interest in CoinGecko, one of the crypto ecosystem's most widely used price-tracking platforms, according to a CoinDesk report. CoinGecko reports 18 million registered users, which makes control of the site a lever over how token prices and market data are presented to a large retail audience. The report gives no deal value, no size for the stake beyond "majority," and no indication of whether CoinGecko's pricing methodology, token listings or editorial policies will change.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** CoinDesk reported that the Solana Foundation has taken a majority interest in CoinGecko, and described CoinGecko as one of the crypto ecosystem's most popular price-tracking platforms.
- **Not yet confirmed:** the deal value, the exact percentage acquired, the structure of the transaction, and any commitments about CoinGecko's independence; the 18 million registered-user figure is CoinGecko's own reported number, and no party outside the report has confirmed the deal's terms.

</div>

## Background

CoinGecko is a price-tracking and market-data aggregator that lists tokens across thousands of crypto assets and reports 18 million registered users; the Solana Foundation is the non-profit that supports development of Solana, a layer-1 blockchain whose tokens and applications depend on such listings for visibility. The stake was announced on November 17 at the Breakpoint conference in London by the foundation's chief product officer, Vibhu Norby, and CoinGecko confirmed the backing without disclosing financial terms, so the size of the investment and the valuation remain unknown.

## Why it matters

CoinGecko's product chief said the plan is to bring Solana-powered trading to CoinGecko's website, GeckoTerminal and the app — adding trade execution to a platform many users treat as a neutral reference for prices and listings. Readers who use its rankings to compare tokens should watch for any change in listing or ranking policy, but neither side disclosed the price or the exact stake, so the size of Solana's influence remains unquantified.

## What to watch

Watch for a statement from CoinGecko or the Solana Foundation, or a regulatory filing, that discloses the stake's size and price, and for any announced change to how CoinGecko sources, labels or ranks market data.
