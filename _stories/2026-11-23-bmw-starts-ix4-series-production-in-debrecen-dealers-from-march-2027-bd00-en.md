<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

BMW has begun series production of the iX4 at its Debrecen plant in Hungary, where the iX3 is already built, making the coupé-styled electric SUV the second Neue Klasse model assembled there. According to electrive, the car will reach dealers from March 2027, initially in two drivetrain variants. The report gives no specifications, battery or range figures, prices, production volumes, or which two variants will be offered first.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** BMW has started series production of the iX4 in Debrecen alongside the iX3, and the model is the second Neue Klasse vehicle built at that plant, electrive reported.
- **Not yet confirmed:** technical specifications, range, pricing, production volumes and which two drivetrain variants will launch; the March 2027 dealer date is BMW's stated plan, and no independent confirmation of output or timing exists yet.

</div>

## Background

BMW's Neue Klasse is its new-generation electric vehicle family, and the Debrecen plant already builds the iX3, the first Neue Klasse model assembled there. BMW's newsroom says the plant began iX4 series production less than two months after introducing 24-hour production, making the iX4 the second Neue Klasse model at the site rather than the opening of a new factory.

## Why it matters

Debrecen now builds two Neue Klasse models on the same line, so buyers who want the iX4's coupé body instead of the iX3's will have to wait for the March 2027 dealer launch and, at least initially, choose from only two drivetrain options.

## What to watch

Watch for BMW to publish iX4 specifications, prices and the names of the two launch variants ahead of the March 2027 dealer rollout.
