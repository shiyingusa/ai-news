Walmart said Kalyan Krishnamurthy, chief executive of its Flipkart unit, India's largest online retailer, will leave the company on December 4, and named Flipkart's chief technology officer as interim CEO, Bloomberg reported on November 25. The report describes Krishnamurthy as a key architect of Flipkart's rise to the No. 1 position in Indian online retail.

The announcement gives no reason for the departure, does not name the CTO serving as interim, and does not set out a timeline for appointing a permanent successor. The change is dated and attributed to Walmart, but the operational details behind it remain undisclosed.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Bloomberg reported that Walmart-owned Flipkart's chief executive, Kalyan Krishnamurthy, plans to leave on December 4, and that the company's chief technology officer will take over as interim CEO. The report describes Krishnamurthy as a key architect of Flipkart's rise to become India's largest online retailer.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The interim CEO is not named, no reason is given for the departure, and there is no timetable or process for appointing a permanent successor. It is also unclear whether the statement came from Walmart or from Flipkart — the headline attributes it to Walmart, while the summary attributes it to Flipkart — and a separate account reported that Flipkart called an emergency town hall on the Monday before the announcement amid speculation about the exit, a detail the company has not confirmed.

</div>

## Background

Flipkart is Walmart's main e-commerce holding in India, and Bloomberg describes Krishnamurthy as a key architect of its rise to become the country's No. 1 online retailer. Putting the chief technology officer in the interim role means a technology executive will run the company while a permanent successor is found; the report gives no timetable for that search.

## Why it matters

Krishnamurthy was the executive most associated with Flipkart's climb to India's largest online retailer, and his exit on December 4 leaves the company under an interim CTO with no named permanent successor. That leadership gap lands while Walmart is still weighing when to list Flipkart: the Economic Times reported Walmart's international CEO saying it was evaluating the right time for an IPO, with Flipkart preparing to move its domicile back from Singapore to India for that purpose. Investors tracking a potential listing should watch whether a permanent CEO is appointed before any filing, since an interim chief during a pre-IPO period complicates diligence and succession messaging.

## What to watch

Watch the December 4 handover and whether Walmart identifies the interim CTO and a timetable for naming a permanent Flipkart CEO.
