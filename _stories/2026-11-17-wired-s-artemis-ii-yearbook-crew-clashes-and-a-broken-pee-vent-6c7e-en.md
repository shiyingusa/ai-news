## Background

Artemis II was NASA's first crewed mission to the Moon since Apollo 17 in 1972 — a roughly 10-day lunar flyby rather than a landing, which NASA framed as a test of deep-space systems ahead of later surface missions. Britannica's mission entry dates the flight to April 1–10, 2026, with four astronauts traveling around the Moon and back. Wired's piece is a yearbook retrospective on that crew, published about seven months after the mission concluded.
