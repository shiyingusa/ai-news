Volkswagen and its energy subsidiary Elli say they will launch their first vehicle-to-grid (V2G) package in Germany in December 2026, with the required DC wallbox priced at €1,995. The package covers VW, VW Commercial Vehicles, Cupra and Skoda.

V2G allows an electric car to discharge power back to the home or grid; Elli has announced tariff details, but the report does not specify them.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Volkswagen and Elli say the German V2G package is due in December 2026, the DC wallbox costs €1,995, and the launch covers VW, VW Commercial Vehicles, Cupra and Skoda, according to the report.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The report does not give the electricity tariff, wallbox power rating, compatible vehicle list, installation cost, or whether grid operators have certified the unit; delivery in December 2026 remains a stated plan.

</div>

## Background

Vehicle-to-grid (V2G) charging lets an electric car's battery push power back into the grid instead of only drawing from it, so the car can earn money at times of high demand. It requires a bidirectional DC wallbox, since the AC chargers built into most EVs only flow one way, and it depends on a grid connection and electricity tariff that allow and pay for exported power — which is why Elli, Volkswagen's energy subsidiary, is selling the wallbox together with a tariff rather than as standalone hardware.

## Why it matters

For VW Group owners, €1,995 sets a retail price for adding V2G, but the economics depend on the undisclosed tariff and on local grid approval, both of which will determine whether discharging the car battery is worth the added hardware cost.

## What to watch

Watch the December 2026 German launch for the final electricity tariff, the compatible-model list and confirmation of installation costs.
