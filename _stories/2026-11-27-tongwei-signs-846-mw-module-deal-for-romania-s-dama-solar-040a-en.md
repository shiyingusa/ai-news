Tongwei and Rezolv Energy have signed an agreement for 846 MW of Tongwei TNC 3.0 modules for the Dama Solar project in Arad County, western Romania. The plant is planned at 1.36 GWp of installed capacity and is expected to generate about 1,800 GWh of electricity a year once operational.

The modules use a quarter-cut cell design that Tongwei says combines high power output with long-term reliability; Tongwei is handling production scheduling and supply coordination from its vertically integrated manufacturing chain. The first truckload of modules has left Tongwei's Nantong facility, which the company describes as the start of deliveries under the agreement — the plant itself is not yet built or generating.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** pv magazine reported that Tongwei and Rezolv Energy signed an 846 MW supply agreement for TNC 3.0 modules, and Tongwei said the first truckload had departed its Nantong plant; the project's 1.36 GWp capacity and Arad County location come from the same report.
- **Not yet confirmed:** the roughly 1,800 GWh annual generation figure is a projection for an unbuilt plant, and module pricing, the delivery schedule, and a construction or commissioning date were not disclosed; the description of Dama Solar as one of Europe's largest single-site solar plants rests on the companies' characterization rather than an independent ranking.

</div>

## Background

Dama Solar is being developed by Rezolv Energy, the independent renewable power producer Actis established in 2022, on a flat site in Arad County that Rezolv describes as being in final development with grid access already confirmed near the site. In September 2026 the European Investment Bank agreed €100 million in financing for the 1.3 GWp plant, which will connect to Romania's high-voltage network. The Tongwei order therefore shifts the project from financing into equipment procurement, with the first truckload already dispatched from Tongwei's Nantong factory.

## Why it matters

The agreement covers modules only, so the 846 MW order secures panel supply but leaves inverters, construction contracts, grid connection and commissioning as separate, undisclosed steps — the practical thing to track for anyone gauging when Dama Solar's 1.36 GWp actually reaches the Romanian grid. It also shows a Chinese manufacturer placing a multi-hundred-megawatt order into an EU utility-scale project, with shipments already moving rather than merely contracted.

## What to watch

Watch for the completion of the remaining module deliveries from Nantong and for Rezolv's construction and grid-connection milestones in Arad County. No delivery completion date or commissioning date was disclosed in the announcement.
