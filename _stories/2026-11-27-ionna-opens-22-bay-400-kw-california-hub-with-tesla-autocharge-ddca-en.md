<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

IONNA has opened a flagship charging site in Westminster, California, with 22 charging bays and peak power of up to 400 kW, according to electrive. The opening coincides with an expansion of automatic charging authentication to Tesla vehicles, so a compatible car identifies itself over the charging cable and a session starts without an app or card.

Support for models from Stellantis brands is scheduled to follow in December. The report does not say which Tesla models are covered, how much power an individual bay delivers when the site is busy, or what the hub charges per kWh.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** electrive reports that IONNA opened a 22-bay site in Westminster, California, with peak power of up to 400 kW, and that automatic charging authentication has been extended to Tesla vehicles, with Stellantis brands to follow in December.
- **Not yet confirmed:** which Tesla models and software versions actually support the automatic authentication, the exact December date for Stellantis brands, whether all 22 bays can deliver 400 kW simultaneously, and the site's pricing. These details rest on IONNA's announcement and were not independently tested.

</div>

## Background

Automatic charging authentication — often branded Autocharge or Plug & Charge — lets a vehicle identify itself to the charger over the cable, so a session starts and bills without an app, card or RFID tag. IONNA runs a US fast-charging network, and this site's launch is paired with extending that automatic authentication to Tesla cars, with models from Stellantis brands to be added in December.

## Why it matters

For Tesla drivers, an IONNA bay can now work like a Tesla Supercharger: plug in and the session starts, with no app, card or screen interaction, which removes the main friction that has kept some owners on Tesla's own network. The practical check is whether a given car has the feature enabled — drivers heading to Westminster should confirm their model is supported before relying on plug-and-charge, and Stellantis owners will have to wait for the December rollout.

## What to watch

Whether IONNA publishes the list of Tesla models and software versions supported by automatic authentication, and whether the promised Stellantis brand support arrives on schedule in December.
