<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in revenue for its fiscal 2027 third quarter, its first earnings report since Jensen Huang's departure, with nearly $20 billion coming from first-quarter production shipments of its Vera Rubin platform. Supply and capacity commitments rose to $390 billion from $279 billion three months earlier, driven mainly by multiyear memory procurement agreements. Nvidia raised its FY2028 revenue growth forecast to 75% from about 70%, and said demand for its AI systems still exceeds available supply. Net income was $65.3 billion at a 74.8% gross margin.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Nvidia reported $115.844 billion in Q3 FY2027 revenue, $65.287 billion in net income, $107.6 billion in data-center revenue, nearly $20 billion from Vera Rubin's first production quarter, and $390 billion in supply and capacity commitments, according to the company's results as reported by Tom's Hardware.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Nvidia's description of Vera Rubin as its fastest product ramp and its claim that hyperscale partners reached production in about one month, twice as fast as Grace Blackwell, are company assertions; the 75% FY2028 growth figure is guidance, and the report does not independently verify how much of the $390 billion in commitments will become delivered capacity.

</div>

## Background

Nvidia's fiscal Q2 filing, as AI Daily Radar's November 16 digest reported, put supply and capacity commitments at $279 billion — the figure that has now climbed to $390 billion. A day before these results, the November 17 digest covered Vera Rubin's MLPerf Training v6.1 debut, where Nvidia said its NVL72 system delivered up to 2x the per-GPU performance of GB300 NVL72; those benchmark results preceded the first quarter of volume shipments reported here. Colette Kress, Nvidia's longtime CFO, became interim CEO on November 12 after cofounder Jensen Huang resigned, according to Nvidia's announcement, making this the first quarterly report in the company's 33-year history delivered without him.

## Why it matters

Because Nvidia says demand still exceeds available supply, AI cloud providers and enterprises—the customer group whose sales grew 195% year over year—should plan for Vera Rubin capacity to remain allocated rather than freely available, even as Nvidia adds substrate and packaging partners.

## What to watch

Nvidia's Q4 FY2027 report, for the quarter ending in late January 2027, will show whether it meets the $69 billion of supply commitments due in the remainder of FY2027 and whether Vera Rubin's fast ramp holds.
