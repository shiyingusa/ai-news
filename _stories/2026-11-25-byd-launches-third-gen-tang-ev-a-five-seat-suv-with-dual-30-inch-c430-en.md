<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

BYD launched the third-generation Tang, a five-seat flagship electric SUV, in China with a starting price below $30,000. The cabin's headline feature is a pair of screens each measuring more than 30 inches.

Electrek's report gives no range, battery capacity, charging figures, trim breakdown or indication of whether the model will be sold outside China. The launch price is the figure BYD is quoting for the Chinese market, not a global one.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Electrek reported that BYD launched the third-generation Tang in China as a five-seat SUV with a starting price below $30,000 and two screens larger than 30 inches; the article lists a 402 hp (300 kW) rear motor and CLTC ranges of 730 km for the base Premium and 850 km for the Laser Premium and Laser Flagship. A separate ievchina listing also gives up to 850 km CLTC, a second-generation Blade Battery and God's Eye B ADAS.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The exact Chinese price, delivery start, battery capacity and all-wheel-drive specifications are not disclosed; Electrek says "launched" while ievchina describes a Q4 launch following a Chengdu Auto Show unveiling, so whether customer deliveries have begun is unclear, and no independent range test or complete specification sheet is available.

</div>

## Background

The Tang is BYD's established flagship SUV nameplate, so a third generation denotes a full model redesign rather than a trim update or facelift. Electrek's report covers only the headline positioning — five seats, two screens measuring over 30 inches, and a China starting price under $30,000 — and does not give range, battery capacity, trim breakdown or delivery timing.

## Why it matters

For Chinese buyers, a five-seat flagship SUV with two screens over 30 inches now starts under $30,000 — but because the report omits range, battery and charging specifications, it is not yet possible to judge what that price buys in driving capability versus rival SUVs. Buyers outside China have no stated availability to plan around.
