<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Google is extending how long it keeps Gemini conversations from 72 hours to two months, and the longer window applies even to users who have turned chat history off, TechCrunch reported on November 17. The change is described as rolling out globally over the coming weeks, so the 72-hour figure still applies in at least some markets until it reaches them. The report is brief and relays no statement from Google itself, and it does not say whether the new window affects chats already deleted or how users can shorten retention themselves.

</div>

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** TechCrunch reports that Gemini chat retention moves from 72 hours to two months, applies worldwide, applies even with history disabled, and rolls out over the coming weeks.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Google's own announcement or help-center documentation of the change; the exact date each market switches over; whether any categories of chats are exempt; and whether content previously slated for deletion within 72 hours is now held longer.

</div>

## Background

Gemini has a chat-history setting that users can switch off so their conversations are not saved to their activity history or used to improve Google's models. Even with that setting off, Google still kept chats for a short period — 72 hours — for safety and abuse review. This change lengthens that window to two months, so turning history off no longer means a conversation disappears within days.

## Why it matters

For Gemini users, switching chat history off no longer means near-immediate deletion: conversations can now sit on Google's servers for roughly 60 days, so anyone who relied on the 72-hour window to limit what is stored — for confidential work queries, for example — should treat the setting as a preference rather than a guarantee of short retention.

## What to watch

Watch Google's Gemini privacy documentation and help-center pages for the updated retention language, which would confirm the two-month window and when the global rollout is complete.
