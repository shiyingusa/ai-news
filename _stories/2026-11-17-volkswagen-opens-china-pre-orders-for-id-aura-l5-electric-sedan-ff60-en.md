FAW-Volkswagen, Volkswagen's main Chinese joint venture, has opened pre-orders in China for the ID. Aura L5, a mid-size electric sedan built on the company's CMP platform. The car is quoted at 580 km of range on the CLTC cycle, China's light-vehicle test standard, which typically yields higher figures than Europe's WLTP or the US EPA cycle. electrive reports the model is expected to launch in 2026; pricing, trim levels and a precise launch or delivery date were not disclosed, so buyers can currently reserve a car whose final specification and cost are not yet public.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** FAW-Volkswagen has opened pre-orders in China for the ID. Aura L5, a mid-size electric sedan on Volkswagen's CMP platform with a quoted 580 km CLTC range, according to electrive and CarNewsChina, which adds that the car uses the CEA 1.4 electrical architecture.
- **Not yet confirmed:** No launch date beyond "2026," no prices and no independent range test are given; the 310 kW power figure and frameless doors come from a single Turkish outlet (DonanımHaber), and the reported 99-yuan deposit credited as 2,000 yuan toward purchase appears only in a Russian report, so treat both as unverified.

</div>

## Background

The ID. Aura L5 was previewed in October 2026, when FAW-Volkswagen teased a China-specific electric sedan on the CMP platform and CEA architecture, and its exterior colours and single- or dual-motor options were shown ahead of the pre-order opening. CMP is Volkswagen's China-market compact electric platform, distinct from the MEB platform used for earlier ID. models sold in Europe and China.

## Why it matters

For Chinese buyers, the pre-order window opened ahead of published pricing and specifications, so a deposit reserves a car whose final cost and equipment are still unconfirmed — anyone ordering should check whether the reservation is refundable and what configuration it locks in. The 580 km CLTC figure is also a test-cycle number, not a measured real-world range, and should be treated as roughly comparable to, but not equal to, a WLTP rating.

## What to watch

Watch for FAW-Volkswagen to publish a price list and a firm launch or first-delivery date for the ID. Aura L5, since both are missing despite the pre-order opening.
