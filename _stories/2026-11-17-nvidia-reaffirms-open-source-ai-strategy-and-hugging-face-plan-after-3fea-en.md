Nvidia's interim CEO has reaffirmed that the company will keep its open-source AI strategy and still intends to acquire Hugging Face, according to TechCrunch, following Jensen Huang's sudden departure. The report frames both the strategy and the acquisition as continuing commitments rather than new initiatives, but gives no price, timeline, financing or regulatory status for a Hugging Face deal, and does not name the interim CEO. No Nvidia filing or deal terms appear in the material available, so the acquisition is a stated intention rather than a completed or signed transaction.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Nvidia’s September 3, 2026 announcement says the company “has agreed to acquire Hugging Face”; TechCrunch reports that Nvidia’s interim CEO reaffirmed the open-source AI strategy and the acquisition plan after Jensen Huang’s departure.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The interim CEO’s identity, the circumstances and timing of Huang’s departure, the deal’s closing date and regulatory conditions, and the $12.9 billion price reported from the earnings call are not established by Nvidia’s announcement; the claims that Nemotron is the most widely used U.S. open model on OpenRouter and that token traffic rose 35x year over year rest on that single report.

</div>

## Background

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's president, CEO and board member effective immediately, and the company named chief financial officer Colette Kress as interim CEO until a permanent successor is chosen, as reported by The Guardian and Notebookcheck. TechCrunch reported the next day that Kress was expected to make changes at Nvidia, particularly in how the company invests in AI startups — dealmaking that had drawn concern from Wall Street. The reaffirmation of the open-source strategy and the reported Hugging Face plan are therefore the first strategy statements attributed to that interim leadership rather than to Huang.

## Why it matters

Hugging Face is the default public hub where developers publish and download open-weight models and datasets, including models from Nvidia's competitors. If Nvidia owns it, those teams would be distributing their work through a channel controlled by the company whose GPUs they also train on, so the terms of hosting, model listings and data access become the practical thing to check — not just whether the models themselves stay open.

## What to watch

Watch for Nvidia's own confirmation of the Hugging Face deal with price, structure and expected close, and for the naming of a permanent CEO to replace Huang. Any antitrust review triggered by a formal agreement, and the company's next scheduled earnings call, are the most likely places for those details to surface.
