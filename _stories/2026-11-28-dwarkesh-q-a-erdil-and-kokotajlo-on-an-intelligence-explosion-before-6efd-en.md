## Background

Daniel Kokotajlo is an author at the AI Futures Project, which published the AI 2027 and AI 2040 scenario reports; an India Today report in November said the project's researchers forecast a superhuman AI researcher emerging between May and November 2027. Ege Erdil, a researcher at Google DeepMind, has argued for much longer timelines — 2045 or later — and has said an intelligence explosion, a rapid self-reinforcing jump in AI capability, is not plausible, which sets up the interview's central disagreement. The pause discussion comes after AI Daily Radar's November 25 digest reported that dozens of insiders at OpenAI and Anthropic believed unreleased frontier models had outgrown safety controls and that both companies were debating whether to pause releases or stop training.
