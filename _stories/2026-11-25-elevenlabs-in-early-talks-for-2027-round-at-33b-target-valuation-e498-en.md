<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

ElevenLabs, which builds AI voice models and voice agents, is in early discussions about a funding round for 2027 that would target a $33 billion valuation, Bloomberg reported, citing unnamed sources. That target is up from the $22 billion valuation the company was reported to carry in September.

The same people put ElevenLabs' annual recurring revenue at $700 million. Nothing is signed: the talks are described as early, no investors, terms or timing beyond the 2027 frame were disclosed, and ElevenLabs has not confirmed the round.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Bloomberg published the report on the talks and the $33 billion target, against a $22 billion valuation reported in September; the figures come from people Bloomberg did not name.
- **Not yet confirmed:** that a round will happen at all, who would invest, its size, its terms or its exact date, and the $700 million annual recurring revenue figure, which rests on the same unnamed sources and has not been independently audited or published by ElevenLabs.

</div>

## Background

ElevenLabs sells access to AI voice models — software that generates and clones speech and runs voice agents — largely on recurring subscriptions. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) annualizes the value of those subscriptions at current rates; it is a snapshot metric, not audited annual revenue. The $22 billion baseline the new target is measured against is the valuation the company carried after its September round, as reported by Bloomberg.

## Why it matters

The numbers set an implied price of roughly 47 times annual recurring revenue, well above what typical enterprise software companies command, so the round would only make sense if investors expect voice AI spending to keep compounding at ElevenLabs' current pace. For companies comparing voice vendors or weighing their own fundraising, the $700 million ARR figure is now the benchmark to argue against — and it is unaudited.

## What to watch

Whether ElevenLabs confirms a round or a term sheet surfaces; an official announcement or regulatory filing would be the first primary evidence, and with the round framed for 2027 the target valuation could move before then.
