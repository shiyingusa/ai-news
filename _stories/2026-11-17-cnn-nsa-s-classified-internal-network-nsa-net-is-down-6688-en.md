CNN reported on November 17, citing six people familiar with the matter, that NSA Net — the highly classified network the National Security Agency uses for its own internal communications — is down. Those sources said there is no evidence so far of a cyberattack.

The report gives no cause, no indication of how much of the network is affected, and no restoration timeline, and the NSA has not publicly confirmed the outage, so the account rests entirely on anonymous sourcing.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** CNN, citing six people familiar with the matter, reports that NSA Net is down and that no evidence of a cyberattack has been found so far.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The NSA has not publicly acknowledged the outage; the cause, the scope of affected systems, the duration and any restoration timeline are unknown, and there is no independent or on-the-record confirmation of CNN's account.

</div>

## Background

NSA Net is the internal network the National Security Agency — headquartered at Fort Meade, Maryland — uses to pass classified information between employees, rather than a public-facing service. That makes reports of an outage a question about the agency's own operational communications, which are normally kept entirely out of public view.

## What to watch

Whether the NSA, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence or CISA issues an official statement confirming the outage and describing its cause, and when NSA Net is reported to be back in service.
