<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Nidus, a startup building AI-powered technology for manufacturing robots used by US defense industrial companies, has raised $30 million across a pre-seed and a seed round, with Kleiner Perkins and Costanoa among its backers. The company says it reached "seven figures" in bookings with defense industrial customers in just four months.

The report does not name customers, disclose how much of the $30 million came from each round, or specify whether those bookings are paid orders or softer commitments, and no deployment or performance figures were provided.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Upstarts Media reports that Nidus raised $30M combined across a pre-seed and a seed round, with Kleiner Perkins and Costanoa among its backers; a Nidus job posting independently describes the company as building AI-and-robotics-based autonomous manufacturing systems.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The "seven figures" in bookings from defense industrial customers within four months is Nidus's own claim, with no customer names, contract values or revenue figures disclosed. The sources also do not say how the $30M splits between the pre-seed and seed, who led either round, or whether the robots are in production deployment rather than pilots.

</div>

## Background

Nidus develops the AI layer for manufacturing robots operated by US defense industrial companies, rather than the robot hardware itself, and its early backers include Kleiner Perkins and Costanoa. The milestone the company cites is bookings — committed customer orders rather than revenue recognized — which it says reached seven figures within four months.

## Why it matters

Defense industrial manufacturers weighing automation now have a newly funded supplier option, but the only evidence of traction is the company's own bookings figure — with no named customers or shipped-unit numbers — so procurement teams should ask for delivery references rather than accept the funding announcement as proof of deployment.
