Sierra, the enterprise customer-experience company co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, said on Monday that it raised $1.75 billion in a Series F round. TechCrunch reported that the round doubled Sierra's valuation to $30 billion within roughly six months, a figure attributed to the report rather than confirmed in the company's own statement.

The report frames the raise as riding demand for AI in customer experience, but it does not name the participating investors, disclose revenue or customer counts, or specify how the new capital will be allocated. No product or capability launch accompanied the funding announcement.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Sierra said on Nov. 23 that it raised a $1.75 billion Series F at a $30 billion valuation, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks, GV and Tiger Global all returning, according to the company's own announcement and CMSWire.
- **Not yet confirmed:** That the valuation doubled within six months (implying a prior round near $15 billion) rests on TechCrunch's reporting rather than a figure in Sierra's announcement; the sources also give no revenue, customer-count or booking details to support the demand claim.

</div>

## Background

CX, short for customer experience, covers the support, service and sales conversations a company has with its customers — the area where Sierra sells AI software, according to the report. Sierra is co-founded by Bret Taylor, a former co-CEO of Salesforce, the large enterprise software vendor whose products include customer service tools. The $30 billion figure is double the valuation the company carried roughly six months earlier, which is why the round is being described as a rapid re-rating rather than a first-time mark.
