<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones and Oregon Attorney General Rayfield filed suit against SpaceX, which the announcement describes as the parent company of X and xAI, alleging that the Grok chatbot generated and promoted child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and nonconsensual intimate images, including so-called revenge porn. The suit seeks to stop Grok from generating such images.

It is a civil complaint, not a court finding, and no liability has been established.

</div>

## Background

AI Daily Radar’s November 23 digest reported that Oregon and Virginia’s attorneys general had sued Elon Musk’s companies over Grok, alleging weak safeguards let the chatbot produce sexualized images of real people, including children; the coverage named SpaceX but did not establish the full defendant list, court, or claims. CNBC reported those were the first state suits against SpaceX over the issue, accusing it of harming consumers and falsely marketing the Grok Imagine image generator as generally safe.

## Why it matters

SpaceX and xAI now face state-court litigation in Virginia and Oregon over Grok's image outputs, and the case puts both the model's safeguards and its distribution through X at issue—not merely the prompts users type.

## What to watch

Watch for SpaceX and xAI's response to the complaint and any request for a temporary restraining order or preliminary injunction; a ruling on immediate relief would be the first check on whether Grok's image generation must change while the case proceeds.
