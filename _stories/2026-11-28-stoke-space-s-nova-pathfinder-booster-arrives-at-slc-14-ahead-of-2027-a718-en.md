Stoke Space's Nova Pathfinder first stage arrived at Space Launch Complex 14 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station on Nov. 22 after a 17-day road trip from Washington, then entered the pad's Horizontal Integration Facility; it is the first rocket at the historic pad since the 1960s. Stoke plans a pad-in-the-loop wet dress rehearsal campaign, after which the stage will roll back to the HIF to have its remaining Zenith engines integrated, return for a static fire, and later mate with the second stage.

The company is targeting an early 2027 inaugural launch, with a Stage 2 static fire scheduled for December at Moses Lake once the upper stage is mated with its Andromeda engine. Four of seven Zenith flight engines have completed acceptance testing, and Stage 2 has completed cryogenic proof testing and a full wet dress rehearsal.

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** NASASpaceflight reported that the first stage arrived at SLC-14 on Nov. 22 and entered the HIF, and that Stage 2 completed cryogenic proof testing and a full wet dress rehearsal; Stoke Space's own X post said a rocket was at SLC-14 for the first time in 60 years.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The early 2027 launch date and the December Stage 2 static fire are Stoke's plans, not completed events; the report gives no payload capacity, price, or independent confirmation that the integrated vehicle will fly on that schedule.

</div>

## Background

Nova is Stoke Space's two-stage, fully reusable rocket, and its upper stage is the unusual part: instead of ablative material or ceramic tiles, it uses an actively cooled metallic heat shield with cryogenic propellant circulated through it during reentry, which the company says is meant to permit repeated flights with minimal inspection between them. SLC-14 itself is a historic Cape Canaveral pad that has not hosted a rocket since the 1960s, so Pathfinder's arrival also reactivates the rebuilt complex. Beyond this first flight, Stoke unveiled a heavier Nova Block 2 variant in September, and that version is expected to begin flying in 2029, according to Payload.

## Why it matters

Stoke's ability to hit the early 2027 target now depends on installing the remaining three Zenith engines, completing Stage 1 static fire and mating the two stages, the steps that gate the first integrated Nova vehicle.

## What to watch

Watch the Stage 2 static fire at Moses Lake in December, then the Stage 1 wet dress rehearsals and static fire at SLC-14 before the early 2027 launch target.
