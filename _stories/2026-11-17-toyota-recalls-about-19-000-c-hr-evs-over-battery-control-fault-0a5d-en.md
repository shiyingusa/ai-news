Toyota is recalling roughly 19,000 C-HR+ electric vehicles worldwide, 1,096 of them in Germany, because of a fault in the battery management system. In the worst case the defect could shut down the electric drivetrain while the car is in motion. The report names the affected model and per-market volume but does not say which model years or build dates are covered, whether the remedy is a software update or a parts swap, or when owners will be notified.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** According to electrive's report, Toyota is recalling roughly 19,000 C-HR+ electric vehicles worldwide, including 1,096 units in Germany, because of a fault in the battery management system that could, in the worst case, shut down the electric drivetrain.
- **Not yet confirmed:** The report is a single trade-press account and does not quote a Toyota recall notice, so the official scope (affected model years and build dates), the fix (software update versus hardware replacement of the battery control unit), the start date of the campaign, and whether any drivetrain shutdowns, crashes or injuries have actually occurred remain unconfirmed.

</div>

## Background

A battery management system (BMS) is the electronics that monitor and control a rechargeable battery pack, managing safe use and long pack life while estimating states such as charge and health. The C-HR+ is Toyota's compact electric SUV on the e-TNGA platform; according to the third-party guide evbattery.us, it is sold with two NMC pack sizes, 57.7 kWh (54 kWh usable) and 77 kWh (72 kWh usable). Neither of those pack figures comes from Toyota, and the recall report does not say which pack or supplier the affected battery control unit belongs to.

## Why it matters

For owners of the affected cars, the failure mode is a sudden loss of drive power rather than a gradual degradation, so the practical action is to watch for Toyota's recall notification and have the battery control unit inspected or reflashed rather than waiting for symptoms to appear.

## What to watch

Toyota's official recall notice should follow with the affected build-date range, the specific remedy, and a notification schedule, none of which this trade report provides.
