<div class="story-lead" markdown="1">

Walmart is rolling out a live implementation of the Agent Gateway it announced last month, and Meta's Muse AI agent is the first shopping agent using it: Muse can search for and purchase Black Friday deals through Walmart's Sparky assistant. Purchases run through Walmart "Agent Profiles," a dedicated space for agent activity inside a customer's Walmart account where shoppers can set spending limits, approve purchases, and review a history of everything the agent bought. Walmart says the gateway lets agents find relevant products without being given the customer's purchase history. The Verge describes the integration as live and rolling out, without specifying how many customers have access or whether it extends past the Black Friday period.

</div>

<div class="fact-check" markdown="1">

## Verified and unconfirmed

- **Verified:** Walmart says it is rolling out a live implementation of its Agent Gateway, that Meta's Muse can find and purchase Black Friday deals via the Sparky assistant, and that Agent Profiles let customers set spending limits, approve purchases and view agent purchase history.
- **Not yet confirmed:** Independent testing of a Muse-initiated purchase, how many customers or which markets have access, whether the integration continues after Black Friday, and whether other AI agents besides Muse are supported on the gateway.

</div>

## Background

Meta introduced Muse in September 2026 as a personal AI agent that acts across apps rather than just answering questions — it was pitched with shopping and browsing capabilities, including comparing prices and placing orders on a user's behalf. Walmart had announced its Agent Gateway the month before this rollout, described as a way for outside AI agents to find relevant products without being handed a shopper's purchase history; Muse is now a live implementation of that gateway, operating through per-agent "Agent Profiles" inside a customer's Walmart account where spending limits and purchase approvals are set.

## Why it matters

For shoppers, the practical control surface is the Agent Profile: spending limits and per-purchase approval are what stand between an agent and a real charge, so those settings should be configured before Muse is given access. For developers building shopping agents, Agent Gateway is the interface to watch, since Walmart says it supplies product discovery without handing over a customer's purchase history — a privacy constraint other retailers may copy.

## What to watch

Whether Walmart extends Agent Gateway beyond the Black Friday window and names additional AI agent partners, or reports how many purchases agents actually completed during the sale.
