China's cumulative installed solar capacity reached about 1.33 TW at the end of October 2026, up 16.3% year on year, according to National Energy Administration (NEA) figures reported by pv magazine. October additions of 12.86 GW were down 5.7% from September, bringing installations for the first ten months of 2026 to roughly 123.69 GW.

Separately, the Jiangsu Provincial Development and Reform Commission published its 2027 mechanism price auction framework for new renewable projects, covering an initial 13 TWh — 12.5 TWh of it for solar and onshore wind — with bids allowed between CNY 0.200 ($0.029)/kWh and CNY 0.391/kWh and a 10-year settlement period. For newly approved utility-scale projects, the volume eligible for mechanism price settlement will be scaled to actual grid-export utilization hours relative to local averages for comparable projects.

## Background

A terawatt is 1,000 gigawatts, so China’s 1.33 TW of cumulative solar capacity equals about 1,330 GW of installed peak generating potential, not actual output. Jiangsu’s “mechanism price” auction is a provincial competitive tender that fixes the settlement price winning renewable projects receive for a set period; the 2027 framework sets a 10-year settlement term and a bidding band of CNY 0.200–0.391/kWh.

## Why it matters

Jiangsu's utilization-hours adjustment means developers bidding into the 2027 auction carry the risk of curtailment themselves: if a plant exports less power than comparable local projects, the share of its output paid at the mechanism price shrinks, which puts a premium on siting and grid-connection quality rather than on bid price alone. The CNY 0.200–0.391/kWh band also gives developers a firm reference for project economics a year ahead.

## What to watch

The NEA said the 72 MW solar plus 30 MW/75 MWh battery project supplying Yongjie Aluminum in Zhejiang is scheduled to begin operations on Dec. 28, a test of whether direct green-power supply can cut an aluminum producer's carbon tariff and electricity costs as projected. Jiangsu's 2027 auction results, including clearing prices within the published band, will show how developers price utilization risk.
