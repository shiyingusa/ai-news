# About AI Daily Radar

AI Daily Radar is a free daily briefing on artificial intelligence. Every morning (US Eastern Time) we publish one issue in English and the same issue in Chinese.

## What we read

Around 80 to 150 new items a day from:

- **AI labs and platforms:** OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Google Research, NVIDIA, Hugging Face.
- **Technology press:** TechCrunch, The Verge, MIT Technology Review, Ars Technica, and Google News results for artificial intelligence.
- **Analysis and research newsletters:** Simon Willison, Import AI, Interconnects, Latent Space.
- **Developer communities:** Hacker News, Reddit (r/MachineLearning, r/LocalLLaMA, r/artificial), GitHub releases of widely used AI projects.
- **Chinese AI media:** QbitAI (量子位) and AI Era (新智元), so English readers also see what is moving in China's AI industry.

## How stories are picked

Each item is scored from 0 to 10 by a large language model against a written rubric: is AI the main subject, is it new, who is affected, how credible is the source. Stories that are not mainly about AI are dropped. Duplicate reports of the same event are merged, and no single type of source may fill the issue. Only the highest-scoring 25 or fewer stories make it in.

## How summaries are written

For each selected story, a language model writes a short summary, background and, where the evidence supports it, the practical impact. It is instructed to separate announced plans from shipped products, attribute opinions and avoid hype. Every story links to its original source, and the score shown beside each headline is the model's rating.

Because the summaries are machine-written, they can contain mistakes. Always check the original source before relying on a detail.

## Corrections and contact

Spotted an error, or want to suggest a source? Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}). We fix confirmed errors and adjust the rules that caused them.

## Advertising

The site is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers have no say in which stories are selected or how they are described. See the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for how ads use cookies.

# 关于 AI 每日雷达

AI 每日雷达是一份免费的人工智能每日简报。每天早上（美东时间）发布一期，同时提供英文版和中文版。

## 我们读什么

每天大约 80 到 150 条新内容，来源包括：

- **AI 实验室与平台：** OpenAI、Google DeepMind、Google Research、NVIDIA、Hugging Face。
- **科技媒体：** TechCrunch、The Verge、MIT Technology Review、Ars Technica，以及 Google 新闻中的人工智能报道。
- **分析与研究通讯：** Simon Willison、Import AI、Interconnects、Latent Space。
- **开发者社区：** Hacker News、Reddit（r/MachineLearning、r/LocalLLaMA、r/artificial），以及常用 AI 开源项目在 GitHub 上的版本发布。
- **中文 AI 媒体：** 量子位、新智元，让读者同时看到中国 AI 行业的动向。

## 怎么选新闻

每条内容由大语言模型按一套成文的评分标准打 0 到 10 分：主题是不是 AI、是不是新消息、影响谁、来源是否可靠。主题不是 AI 的内容会被剔除；同一事件的多篇报道会合并；单一类型的来源不能占满一期。每期最多收录得分最高的 25 条。

## 摘要怎么写

每条入选新闻由大语言模型撰写简短摘要、背景，以及有证据支撑时的实际影响。写作规则要求区分"宣布的计划"和"已经上线的产品"，标明观点出处，不夸大。每条都附原始来源链接，标题旁的分数是模型的评分。

摘要由机器生成，可能有错误。引用具体细节前，请先核对原文。

## 更正与联系

发现错误，或想推荐信息源？请发邮件到 [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }})。确认的错误我们会更正，并调整导致错误的规则。

## 广告

本站的运营费用来自 Google AdSense 广告。广告主不能影响新闻的选择和描述。广告如何使用 Cookie，请见[隐私政策]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})。
