# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 15, 2026*

AI Daily Radar ("we") runs aidailyradar.com. This page explains what data is collected when you visit.

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. Apart from the optional newsletter below, we do not collect your name, email or other personal details through this site. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page views. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and gives us only aggregate numbers such as page views, referring sites and countries.

Your language choice (English or Chinese) is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.

## Advertising and cookies

This site includes Google AdSense code and may show ads served by Google. Our AdSense account is under review, so ads may not appear yet.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform, which supports the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. You can consent, decline, or choose which purposes and partners to allow.

## Newsletter {#newsletter}

If you subscribe to the Morning Brief, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy), and used only to send you the newsletter. Buttondown may record whether emails are opened or links are clicked. You will receive a confirmation email first, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. We do not sell or share subscriber lists.

## Comments and saved stories {#comments}

If you comment, giscus shows your GitHub username and avatar with your comment, and the comment is stored in our public GitHub repository under GitHub's privacy policy. Stories you save with the Save button are kept only in your own browser; we never receive that list.

## Links to other sites

Every story links to its original source. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
